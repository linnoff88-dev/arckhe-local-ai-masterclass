📰 The world changed this week

This isn’t headline hype: access to cutting-edge intelligence is becoming a controlled privilege. The most capable models start becoming available first to selected groups — partner companies, governments, “early access.” Anyone not on the list gets left behind just when those on it have the biggest advantage.

🆕 New here?

"Frontier model" is the most capable LLM right now — the top of the line (like the latest version of Opus, GPT, or Gemini). "LLM" is the AI model that runs behind ChatGPT: in practice, a giant file of “weights” + a program that generates text from it.

🔑 The central idea

Three forces add up at the same time:

  • •Restricted access: the best models reach a few people first.
  • •Global race: labs around the world (including Chinese ones, such as GLM, Qwen, and DeepSeek) are releasing increasingly powerful open models.
  • •Dependency: using only the cloud leaves you at the mercy of other parties' pricing, policies, and availability.

📊 What to watch

  • Open models are improving month by month — the gap with the closed top models is shrinking.
  • “Invite-only access” announcements and waitlists for the newest models.
  • More countries and companies launching their own models — a sign that this has become a strategic issue.

💸 Hardware is scarce and expensive

The second driver of the shift is physical: memory and GPUs are getting expensive and hard to come by. And the pressure is likely to get worse—because robots, drones, self-driving cars, and AI data centers will consume memory on a scale that hasn't even started yet.

1

Prices rising

RAM, VRAM, and premium graphics cards have gotten more expensive—and prices keep rising more than they used to.

2

Exploding demand

Every humanoid robot, drone, and self-driving car needs a lot of memory. These products have barely started being manufactured — when production scales up, the demand for memory will grow.

3

Today's window

Good hardware is still expensive, though obtainable. The thesis bet: build your lab while you still can.

💡 Practical tip

You no you need to spend a fortune to get started. Starting with the computer you already have is a legitimate strategy — and in tracks 2 and 3, you'll see exactly what fits on your machine. The urgency is about start, not about buying everything.

🛡️ The way out is sovereignty

If access can be restricted and the hardware can become inaccessible, the answer is to stop depending on it. AI sovereignty is simple to define: the intelligence runs on your machine, under your control, and no one can shut it down.

🎯 What changes when it’s local

  • •Your data doesn’t travel to a third-party server.
  • •No one can change the price, cut off access, or block you by region.
  • •Works without internet — the intelligence lives with you.

💡 Practical tip

"Sovereign" doesn't mean "alone against the world." It means having a a base that’s yours and use the cloud when it makes sense—not because you have no choice. It’s freedom to choose, not dogma.

🎁 What you get

The three pillars of local AI — private, unlimited, and non-removable — they’re not marketing. Each enables things the cloud simply doesn’t allow. Compare:

✓ Local AI

  • ✓Prompts and responses stay on your machine
  • ✓Unlimited use — only pay for electricity
  • ✓Works offline, with no rate limit
  • ✓Can’t be cut or blocked

✗ Cloud only

  • ✗Your data passes through third-party servers
  • ✗Per-token toll—expensive at scale
  • ✗Rate limits and outages beyond your control
  • ✗Price, policies, and access change without warning

🔓 The benefit few people see

Because it’s unlimited and always on, local access unlocks use cases impossible in the cloud due to cost: agents running 24/7, continuous scans, monitors that never sleep. It’s the topic of Track 4—and the biggest surprise for anyone who only knows subscription chat.

🧱 Myths that hold you back

Three beliefs stop most people at the door. Let’s dismantle each one — because they cost you the opportunity to get started.

✗ The myth

  • ✗"I need to spend a fortune on hardware"
  • ✗"A local model is too dumb to be useful"
  • ✗"It'll become obsolete in 2 years"

✓ The reality

  • ✓You can get started with the laptop in the closet and a small model
  • ✓For most tasks, “good enough” is already here
  • ✓More efficient models get more out of old hardware — the cycle has changed

💡 Practical tip

The best way to bust a myth is to test it. By the end of this module, you'll be able to check your machine; in Track 3, you'll run your first model in minutes. Experience is worth more than opinion.

⌨️ Practical example: check your machine

Objective: see how much memory you have — the number that determines which model fits. Paste this into the terminal.

# Linux
free -h
# macOS (total RAM)
system_profiler SPHardwareDataType | grep "Memory"
# Windows (PowerShell)
systeminfo | findstr /C:"Total Physical Memory"

How to check: note the total RAM (e.g., 16 GB). In Track 2, you’ll use this number to choose the right model. If you have an NVIDIA GPU, also run nvidia-smi to check the VRAM.
